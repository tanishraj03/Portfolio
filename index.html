Founder chapter · Raahe

Every dream deserves a stage. We built them.

India watches more live performance than almost anywhere on earth, yet the people making it struggle to pay rent. In college, Smera Shetty and I started Raahe to build the missing piece: nights where new artists get a real stage, clients who actually pay them, and a crowd that turns up for both.

No follower count, no showreel, no contacts. If you can hold a room, you're in. That rule turned into Bengaluru's top-ranked open mic and an ecosystem for singers, comics, poets and bands.

What I owned

  • Client relations and the P&L for community events and artist management, bootstrapped to ~₹30L in revenue.
  • 25+ venue alliances, including SOCIAL, Starbucks, Forum South Bengaluru and Obeya.
  • Launched the Raahe Open Mic IP and ran every brand collab, taking it to #1 for Open Mic on Zomato District in Bengaluru.
  • Venue pitch that works: we bring 100–150 people through the door in a couple of hours, and they stay to eat and drink.
